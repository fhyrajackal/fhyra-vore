    Leave your last words. They'll be engraved beside your name in The Digested, forever.
    How it works
    • Struggle spends a d20 roll of stamina to protect your health for 2d20 seconds.
    • Worship protects your stamina for 1d20 seconds and pleases Fhyra a lot. Worshipping while exhausted gives you a second wind (+8 stamina).
    • Beg rolls a d20. A natural 20 earns instant relief, a natural 1 is just pathetic, anything else lets Fhyra grant or deny you.
    • Squirm gives you 10 seconds to tap as fast as you can. Every squirm pleases her and tires you.
    • All four share a 30s cooldown. At 0 stamina you're exhausted: you can't struggle and health drains faster.
    • When Fhyra's pleasure hits 100%, she can Savor you (a breather) or Devour you (a surge of digestion). Choose how you please her wisely.
    • Extra tributes paid during the game restore health: +1 per $5.

    How it works
    • Chamber I: their health and stamina slowly drain. There's no digestion yet. Swallow them deeper, or pull them back out and choose another route.
    • Chamber II: everything drains faster and digestion rises at your chosen rate. Clench and Insta-Churn become available.
    • Tease (Chamber I) drains 6 stamina. Rub (Chamber II) adds 2% digestion but soothes 3 stamina. Both please you and have a 12s cooldown.
    • When the preything begs, you have 30 seconds to grant or deny them.
    • At 100% pleasure you can Savor or Devour. Pleasure slowly fades while it's below 100%.
    • Confirming an extra tribute mid-game heals them by +1 health per $5 and pleases you.
